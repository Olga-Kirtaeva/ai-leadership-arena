No open-source or Creative Commons license has been selected yet. Before any
public release, licensing will be evaluated separately for:

- experimental source code;

- research datasets and generated logs;

A future license decision will apply only when it is stated explicitly in the
relevant file or directory. Until then, all rights remain reserved.
